Audit your past decisions, track outcomes, and identify patterns of success and failure.

---

## Steps

**1. Find decisions** — use `obsidian-vault` MCP to search notes with `type: decision` in frontmatter.

**2. Find outcomes** — for each decision, look for `outcome:` field or mentions in later notes.

**3. Calculate and display:**
```
DECISION OUTCOMES

Recent:
  ✓ GOOD    — [Decision A] (3 mo ago) — [result]
  ✓ GOOD    — [Decision B] (2 mo ago) — [result]
  ⚠ MIXED   — [Decision C] (1 mo ago) — [result]
  ⏳ PENDING — [Decision D] (2 wk ago)

SUCCESS RATE BY CATEGORY
  Technical:  [N]%  ([x]/[y] good)
  Business:   [N]%
  Hiring:     [N]%

PATTERNS OF ERROR
  - [Pattern observed] → [failure rate]
  - [Pattern observed] → [failure rate]
```

**4. Recommend:**
```
SUGGESTIONS
  1. [Actionable based on patterns]
  2. Review [N] pending decisions
  3. Add outcome to: [[note-x]], [[note-y]]
```

**5.** Offer to open any decision note for review or outcome update.
