Resurface notes you haven't visited in 30+ days, ranked by relevance to your current context.

---

## Steps

**1. Find old notes** — use `obsidian-vault` MCP to get notes not modified in 30+ days.

**2. Score relevance** for each (0–100%):
- Linked by other notes (importance) → up to 40%
- Keyword overlap with your 10 most recent notes → up to 35%
- Keyword overlap with active projects → up to 25%

**3. Show top 5:**
```
📚 Notes you haven't seen in 30+ days:

HIGH RELEVANCE
  92% — [[note-title-a]] (47 days ago)
  87% — [[note-title-b]] (62 days ago)

MEDIUM RELEVANCE
  71% — [[note-title-c]] (38 days ago)
  65% — [[note-title-d]] (55 days ago)
  58% — [[note-title-e]] (91 days ago)

Open one? [note name / skip]
```

**4. If user names a note** — read it via MCP and show the content in context. Ask if they want to update, link, or capture a follow-up note.
